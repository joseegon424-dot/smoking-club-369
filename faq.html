WHAT IS A SOCIAL CLUB?
A social club is a private, members-only association where adults can safely and responsibly enjoy cannabis in a legal, non-commercial setting. We operate under Spanish law and focus on community, education, and harm reduction.
WHO CAN JOIN THE SOCIAL CLUB?
Membership is open to adults aged 21 and over with valid ID. While local residents are preferred, we may accept visitors with prior registration and adherence to club rules.
HOW DO I BECOME A MEMBER AT YOUR SOCIAL CLUB?
To join our social club, you’ll need to register in person or online, present a valid ID, and agree to our club guidelines. Some clubs may require a referral, but we also allow direct sign-ups in certain cases.
WHAT ARE THE COSTS FOR A MEMBERSHIP?
We charge €15,- per year for a membership.
WHAT CAN I EXPECT INSIDE THE CLUB?
Expect a relaxed, friendly atmosphere with comfortable seating, music, games, and a curated selection of cannabis strains and products. It’s a safe space to chill, connect, and learn.
